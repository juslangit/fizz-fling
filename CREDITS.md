# Credits

**Fizz Fling** — made by Luqman Hakeem.

## 3D models
Every model (bottle, cap, picnic table, trees, bushes, clouds, signs, flag, fence, rock) was
built in Blender from code: `tools/blender/build_assets.py`. The bottle label is drawn by
`tools/blender/make_label.py`. No third-party models.
