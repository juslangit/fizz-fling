## Description
Shake your phone as hard as you can to build up the fizz in a soda bottle, then tilt it to
aim — and POP! The cap blasts down the park field and the soda sprays out after it.

Your score is how far the cap flies **plus** how far the spray reaches.

- **Solo:** beat your own best distance.
- **Party:** pass one phone round 2–6 friends, 1 or 3 rounds, best throw wins.
- Wind changes every turn — adjust your aim. About 40° flies furthest.

**Play it on a phone.** It uses your phone's motion sensors. On iPhone, tap PLAY FULL SCREEN
and allow motion access when asked. No motion sensor? Rub the screen fast to shake, and
drag to aim (or mash Left/Right and use the arrow keys on a computer).

Made with Godot. Every 3D model built in Blender. Sounds CC0 (Freesound, Kenney).
